If you work with LLMs or embeddings and haven't heard of superposition, this one is for you. 👇

LLMs work with embeddings of only a few thousand dimensions. Yet they seem to represent far more concepts than that. So where does it all fit?

The surprising answer: they don't give each concept its own dimension. They share. 🧩

Concepts get packed into overlapping directions, so they interfere with each other. That sounds like it should break the model. Mostly, it doesn't, and the reasons are two of the most elegant ideas in ML interpretability:
💬 how language uses concepts
📈 how much room high-dimensional space really has

We built an interactive explainer where you don't just read about superposition, you discover it: train a tiny neural network in your browser and watch it learn to pack features, one guided experiment at a time. The video gives a glimpse.
