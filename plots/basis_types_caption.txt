Three types of vector bases and their induced coordinate systems in ℝ². Each panel overlays the standard Cartesian grid (light grey, background) with the coordinate grid defined by the two basis vectors ᵒ₁ (red) and ᵒ₂ (blue). The custom grid axes are drawn in black; intermediate grid lines in grey. Left – Arbitrary basis: the vectors are neither orthogonal nor unit-length, producing a sheared, non-uniform grid. Centre – Orthogonal basis: the vectors are perpendicular but have different lengths, giving a rectangular grid that is not square. Right – Orthonormal basis: the standard basis rotated 15° clockwise, preserving both perpendicularity and unit length and yielding a congruent but rotated copy of the standard grid.
