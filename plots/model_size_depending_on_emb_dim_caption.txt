Total parameter count of a GPT-style dense decoder transformer as a function of its embedding / model dimension d_model, for five prominent architecture families. Parameter count is approximated as P ≈ 12·L·d² + (V + n_ctx)·d, where L is the number of transformer layers, V the vocabulary size, and n_ctx the context length. The y-axis uses a logarithmic scale. Because P grows quadratically with d, even modest increases in embedding dimension incur a steep cost in parameters. Reference points mark the published widths of GPT-3 175B, LLaMA-2 70B, GPT-2 XL, LLaMA-2 7B / Mistral 7B, and BERT-Large.
